### Blank phone
A single realistic modern compact Android smartphone shown exactly straight-on, full handset including rounded outer graphite frame, transparent surrounding background. Portrait product cutout. The screen must be perfectly flat white/light ivory, entirely BLANK with no icons, no writing, numbers, graphs or mockups drawn on it.
